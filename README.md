**Vice Cover** turns your photo and a caption into GTA-style cover art in the style of GTA 6's posters: a multi-panel poster (Hero + 2, Grid 4, or Strip 4 layouts) with bold gradient caption lettering and a warm Vice City sunset / neon colour grade — halftone dots, vignette, and a procedural sunset scene for any panel without a photo yet.

Add your own photos from your machine, crop and position each panel (click a panel, drag it, scroll to zoom, or use the sliders), fill several panels with one photo or several, edit the title and subtitle, then export a PNG in the common post sizes: 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350, or 1920 × 1080. Everything runs offline in the browser — photos are never uploaded. Three original sample photos are bundled under `app/samples/` so you can try it in one click. No Rockstar Games logo, artwork, or font is used anywhere.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome.
